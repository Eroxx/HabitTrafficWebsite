Privacy Policy

Habit Traffic collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, and no server of mine for your habits to sit on.

Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version. Your habits live on your device and in your own iCloud account. I cannot see them. Nothing is sent to any server I run, because there isn't one. No tracking, no ads, no analytics, no third-party kits.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in Habit Traffic — your habits, categories, completion history, streaks, notes, and any sounds you record — is stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, that same information syncs through your own private iCloud database so it can reach your other devices and your Apple Watch. This uses Apple's CloudKit. The data sits in your iCloud account, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and I have no access to it. I cannot read it, export it, or recover it for you.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app simply keeps everything on the device.

Microphone

Habit Traffic can record a custom sound to play when you complete a habit. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and only after you grant permission. The recording is saved with your app data and never leaves your device and iCloud account. It is not transcribed, analysed, or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. There is no push server. Nothing about what you are reminded of leaves your phone or watch.

Files on your device

If you turn on the planning integration in Settings, the app writes small files describing which habits are due into a folder in your own iCloud Drive, so another app of yours can read them. Those files stay in your account.

What Habit Traffic never does

  • No account to create, and no email or password collected.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The app contains no third-party code of that kind at all.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health or calendar access.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because nothing is collected to sell or share.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

The statistics and insights you see in the app are calculated on your device from your own history.

Links out of the app

Habit Traffic links to a Discord community and to an email address. If you follow the Discord link you are on Discord, under their privacy policy, not mine.

Children

Habit Traffic is a personal habit tracker suitable for any age, and it collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings has Delete all data, which permanently removes your habits, categories and history from the device and from your iCloud database. Deleting the app removes the local copy; to clear the iCloud copy as well, use Delete all data first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version appears on this page.

Contact
